There were over twenty players each with a level 20 character fighting in teams of two squared off against other teams of two initially. They did, however, have the option of invading other fighting arenas and only the last one standing in the end won. My partner was blitzed and killed in the very first round (he was not much of an optimizer and didn't take my advice when making his build). This forced me to square off against a level 20 Warlock and Monk by myself. Fortunately for myself, I was playing a level 18 Forge Cleric / level 2 Paladin Warforged. With a single cast of Anti-Magic Field upon myself, I managed to begin wiping the floor with the both of them. This unfortunately drew the attention of the other participants who immediately realized the existential threat I posed and led to me being targeted by several other teams who agreed to a truce to take me out. The Fiend Warlock managed to bypass my defenses by hurtling me through hell and I lost concentration on my Anti-Magic field. Before I could reapply it, I got afflicted with Feeblemind which rendered me essentially helpless. In order to save myself, I instinctually dove into a nearby pit of lava. Being a Forge Cleric Warforged, I was not only immune to lava but didn't need to breath so I could stay in it indefinitely. However, this did not mean I was safe. Several high level casters continued trying to kill me by strafing the lava pool with elemental magic. I endured until they decided to simply trap me underneath by using Cone of Cold to solidify the surface. However, this ultimately saved my life.
